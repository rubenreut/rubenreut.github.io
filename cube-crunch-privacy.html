Cube Crunch Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026

Cube Crunch ("the app", "we", "us") is a puzzle game made by Ruben Reut, an independent developer based in Ireland. This policy explains what information is collected when you play, why, and the choices you have. The app has no accounts and no sign-in.

1. Information stored on your device only

Your scores, best score, selected skin, unlocked skins, settings (sound, music, vibration) and saved game progress are stored only on your device. We do not receive or upload this information. Deleting the app deletes it.

2. Advertising

Cube Crunch is free and is paid for by ads. Ads are delivered through AppLovin MAX, which runs an auction between ad networks including Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, these partners may collect:

  • device information such as model, operating system version, language and screen size;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it (country or city level, not precise GPS location);
  • identifiers for advertising: the Identifier for Vendors (IDFV) and, only if you allow tracking, Apple's Advertising Identifier (IDFA);
  • information about the ads you see and interact with (impressions, clicks, rewarded ads watched).

This is used to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, where you have agreed, show ads more relevant to you. Each partner processes this information under its own privacy policy:

  • AppLovin: applovin.com/privacy
  • Google: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and policies.google.com/privacy

3. Your choices

  • Tracking (iOS): the app asks for permission before your IDFA can be used. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, through a consent screen. You can change your choice later from the app's settings. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalised.
  • Rewarded ads (for example, watching an ad to revive) are always optional.

4. Legal basis (GDPR)

For players in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, personalised advertising relies on your consent. Non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and keeping the app working rely on our legitimate interest in offering a free, ad-supported game.

5. Children

Cube Crunch is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with anyone other than the advertising partners named above, which receive it directly from your device when ads are shown.

7. Retention and security

We do not keep a copy of your information. Advertising partners keep data according to their own policies linked above.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information, and to complain to your data protection authority (in Ireland, the Data Protection Commission). Because we do not hold your data ourselves, requests about ad data are best sent to the partners above; we are happy to help you do this.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new effective date.
